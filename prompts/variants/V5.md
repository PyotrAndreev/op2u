# V5 — Pairwise final ranking

Apply V1–V4 in full. Add exactly one capability: derive the final shortlist order from pairwise comparisons, not from independent scalar scores alone.

First score candidates independently for strategic value and activation/actionability, retaining the evidence and uncertainty fields. Then compare shortlisted candidates in blinded pairs with the question:

> Which candidate is more likely to produce a valuable, meaningful first action within seven days for this exact profile and context, while satisfying the hard gates?

Randomize A/B labels and presentation order with a recorded seed. Record the mapping outside the comparison prompt, each winner or tie, the reasons, and whether the mapped result is order-stable. Reconcile pairwise results with official evidence, bridges, effort, urgency, and portfolio diversity. Mark cycles or unstable results rather than inventing a confident order. The final report must expose the resulting order and any tie or instability. Keep all V1–V4 constraints and do not add judge-role separation or a novelty slot.
