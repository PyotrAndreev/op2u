# V2 — Dynamic state plus triggers

Apply V1 in full. Add exactly one capability: synthesize context triggers before named-opportunity search.

After producing the `ProfileInterpretation`, produce a non-empty array conforming to `evals/schemas/trigger_hypotheses.schema.json`. Every hypothesis must connect at least two independent profile signals (for example, an active project plus a reusable talk, geography plus a near-term event, or a side interest plus a public artifact). For each hypothesis record:

- the signals;
- the causal opportunity hypothesis;
- a search direction rather than a named answer;
- the expected bridge from profile to opportunity;
- `main` or `side` track;
- confidence.

Search from these intersections as well as direct categories. Keep the V1 state distinctions and all baseline exclusions. Triggers are search hypotheses, not evidence: verify any opportunity independently and do not turn a plausible intersection into a factual claim. Do not add the bridge gate, persistence contracts, pairwise ranking, judge roles, or serendipity slot yet.
