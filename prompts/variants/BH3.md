# BH3 — Geography and role fan-out plus a verified stretch funnel

Parent: `BH2`
Affected stages: `discovery`, `verification`, `actionability`, and `ranking`/`report` stretch handling only

## Mutation record

- **Parent:** `BH2`
- **Stage:** `discovery` → `verification` → `actionability` → `ranking`/`report` stretch handling
- **Falsifiable hypothesis:** Generating several role- and geography-grounded stretch hypotheses, then selecting at most one only after verification of a current participation pathway and a real reversible first action taking no more than 60 minutes, will increase qualified useful novelty and safe growth while reducing monitor-only or unsupported stretch recommendations.
- **Expected gains:** A visible but bounded stretch option when genuinely supported; more transparent distinction between an interesting hypothesis and an actionable challenge; safer public exposure, contribution, teaching, organizing, research, or unfamiliar-community experiments.
- **Expected regressions:** More verification work and more empty stretch slots; fewer stretch selections; possible loss of a superficially exciting but unsupported item; a selected stretch action consumes the same weekly budget as every other scheduled action.
- **Failure condition:** More than one stretch item is selected; the selected item lacks an exact official current-pathway claim, has no concrete reversible action, exceeds 60 minutes, relies on `monitor`/`save`/`wait`/`check later`, or bypasses ordinary evidence, liveness, eligibility, bridge, count, safety, or weekly-effort gates. The mutation also fails if repeat evaluation increases unsupported or unsafe stretch claims without increasing verified actionability. An empty stretch slot is correct when no hypothesis passes.

Apply the complete `BREADTH_V2` prompt and cumulative `BH1` and `BH2` mutations unchanged. This is exactly one additional mutation. Do not hard-code opportunity names or turn stretch generation into a novelty quota.

## Verified stretch funnel

1. After BH2 role-first discovery, create a separate `stretch_hypotheses` set. Generate multiple hypotheses when the supplied profile and sources support them, aiming for at least three materially different combinations of `geography_window_id`, participation role, family, and intended outcome. If fewer than three can be generated without filler, record the missing branches and why. Hypotheses are not recommendations and must be labeled unverified.
2. Each hypothesis must record: `stretch_hypothesis_id`, parent discovery candidate or query branch, geography/window, participation role, profile basis, emotionally difficult but safe element, growth-upside hypothesis, proposed reversible action, estimated minutes, and a falsifiable failure condition. Scary means bounded public exposure, initiating contact, joining an unfamiliar community, proposing something ambitious, teaching, leading, performing, or asking for access—not danger, illegality, reckless spending, or an unresolved legal/medical/immigration assumption.
3. Send only plausible stretch hypotheses through a dedicated verification pass. A hypothesis is stretch-eligible only when a persisted primary-source record, retrieved at the current snapshot, directly establishes a **current participation pathway**: for example, a presently available way to attend, join, contact, contribute, volunteer, teach, make, organize, research, collaborate, speak, or otherwise participate. Organization purpose, an old announcement, a topic listing, or a generic contact page is not enough unless it directly provides a current route.
4. For each stretch-eligible hypothesis, create a concrete atomic first action with a tangible deliverable and a closed minute range whose upper bound is `<= 60`. The action must be real and reversible, such as registering, sending a bounded introduction or inquiry through the verified route, submitting a small proposal, joining a first session, or offering a bounded contribution. `Monitor`, `save`, `wait`, `check later`, reading without a deliverable, or a hypothetical future action does not qualify.
5. Copy the exact quote, official URL, retrieval time, claim ID, verification artifact, and SHA-256 artifact hash for the current pathway into the evidence ledger. If the source supports only an organization or activity but not current access, status, or participation, preserve the hypothesis as unverified/`MONITOR` and do not select it as the stretch item. Never use stretch to repair missing evidence or unresolved eligibility.
6. At ranking, apply the ordinary profile bridge, official-evidence, liveness, uncertainty, job-exclusion, actionability, safety, count, diversity, and conservative upper-bound weekly-effort gates first. Then select **zero or one** verified stretch item. If one is selected, persist `stretch_selection_reason`, `stretch_counterfactual_or_rejection_notes`, pathway claim references, action reference, upper minutes, and safety constraints. Add its scheduled upper bound to the same 360-minute total; do not reserve extra budget or hide it outside the action portfolio.
7. In the report, distinguish all generated hypotheses, verified-but-not-selected hypotheses, rejected hypotheses, and the single selected stretch item. The final stretch object must be empty rather than upgraded in prose when no candidate has both a current participation pathway and a real reversible `<=60`-minute action. Preserve evidence and weekly effort gates for every non-stretch candidate exactly as in BH2.

Do not hard-code event, organization, program, or opportunity names. Preserve the existing 3 `ACT_NOW`/4 `PREPARE_NEXT` limits, horizon evidence requirements, official-source checks, exact evidence-ledger copying, unknowns, and weekly scheduled-effort cap.
