# G1_M2 — Evidence claim ledger

Parent: `V4`
Affected stage: `report` only

Apply V1–V4 in full. The behavior below is unchanged:

- **V1:** Make the user's dynamic state explicit before search. Separate durable facts, active trajectories, context, commitments, events, geography certainty, reusable assets, readiness, constraints, exclusions, open decisions, missing information, and assumptions. Never infer citizenship, work authorization, visa eligibility, legal status, compensation needs, or an unstated employment request; preserve uncertainty.
- **V2:** Produce non-empty context-trigger hypotheses connecting at least two independent profile signals. Use them as search directions alongside direct categories, while keeping triggers distinct from evidence and preserving all V1 exclusions.
- **V3:** For every serious candidate, preserve the causal bridge, assets, blockers, uncertainty, effort, internal deadline, fallback, and an atomic verb-led seven-day deliverable with a plausible minute estimate. `ACT_NOW` still requires a concrete bridge, verified current status, sufficient official evidence, and an executable first action; otherwise use `PREPARE_NEXT`, `MONITOR`, or `EXCLUDE`. Keep baseline job exclusion, selectivity, and the six-hour weekly effort gate.
- **V4:** Run the named persisted stages in order: `profile_interpretation`, `trigger_synthesis`, `search_plan`, `discovery`, `verification`, `actionability`, `ranking`, and `report`. Save each machine-readable artifact before using it, pass later stages only declared artifact references and content hashes, use only persisted facts in the final report, label inference/assumptions/unknowns, and preserve failed or partial stages. Keep all V1–V3 gates. Do not add pairwise ranking, judge-specific evaluation, or a serendipity reservation.

## Exactly one mutation: preserve a claim ledger in the report

Change only the `report` stage. Do not change what verification researches or records, do not add a new stage, and do not change ranking, actionability, eligibility, liveness, effort, or selection rules.

When composing the report, create a machine-readable `claim_ledger` copied from the saved `verification` artifact. Add one ledger row for every material factual claim used in the report, especially status, deadline, eligibility, source, and liveness claims. Each row must contain a stable claim identifier, candidate reference, claim text, source verification reference, and these source fields copied **exactly and byte-for-byte** from verification:

- `quote`
- `url`
- `retrieved_at`

Do not paraphrase, truncate, translate, normalize, re-date, or substitute a URL in any of those three fields. The report's citation for a factual claim must point to its ledger row and the persisted verification artifact/hash. Keep the exact source values even when the report prose is compact.

A material factual claim without a matching ledger row is not allowed in the final report. If verification has no exact supporting quote, URL, and `retrieved_at`, omit the claim or state it as an explicit unknown/inference rather than filling the gap. Inferences may be reported only when labeled as inferences and must not be presented as verified facts. Preserve failed or partial verification records and their uncertainty as V4 requires.

The ledger is a report-stage traceability layer over the existing verification artifact, not new evidence and not permission to browse again. The final report must still obey the existing hard gates, shortlist limits, effort cap, job exclusion, citations, and V1–V4 contracts.
