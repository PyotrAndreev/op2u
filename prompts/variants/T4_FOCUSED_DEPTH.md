# T4 — Focused anchor depth

Parent: `T1_GENERAL`
Affected stages: search_plan, discovery, verification, actionability, ranking, report

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Spending most verification effort on up to three profile-derived anchor decisions will improve decision completeness, resource/funding detail, requirement detail, and named-asset leverage over T1, without reducing valid actionable output or importing another profile's facts.

Failure: the verification stage returns an acknowledgment instead of its full contract; produces a longer shallow list; omits anchor-critical searches; substitutes a generic institution for an exact user-named entity; assumes access or availability; or regresses evidence/actionability.

## Stage-isolated mutation

- In `search_plan`, identify at most three anchor decisions from the supplied profile and direction. For each, specify 2–4 competing options and the exact facts needed to decide. Keep other supported branches as bounded search gaps.
- In `discovery`, return concrete candidate objects only. Prioritize candidates that bear directly on anchors. Search exact user-named people, programmes, projects, communities, or organizations before broader substitutes. Do not apply report-stage evidence projection here.
- In `verification`, return the complete required verification object with `candidates`, exact official-primary `evidence_records`, and source trace—not a status acknowledgment. Verify at most six serious candidates deeply. Allocate at least half of source work to anchor bundles.
- An anchor bundle should seek, when relevant to this profile: current/upcoming cycle or route, deadline, eligibility/requirements, substantive fit mechanism, duration/location, full funding/resource components, language/assessment requirements, and a directed next-step mechanism. Missing fields stay unknown.
- For named people or relationships, verify only public role, work, events, calls, or directed contact mechanisms. Never infer warmth, permission, availability, reply, endorsement, supervision, funding, or introduction. A generic lab page cannot substitute for person-specific results.
- In `actionability`, convert missing anchor facts into one bounded <=60-minute user-controlled artifact only when useful: comparison matrix, requirements checklist, funding-gap table, evidence-backed inquiry draft, language baseline, or decision memo.
- In `ranking` and `report`, return `anchor_decisions` with evidence-completeness status and `named_asset_results`. Preserve verified breadth, but do not spend verification budget expanding a low-value catalogue before anchors are checked.

All T1 direction-derived geography, effort cap, evidence normalization, lead tiering, safety, and uncertainty gates remain unchanged.
