# V6 — Evidence-grounded judges

Apply V1–V5 in full. Add exactly one capability: evaluate saved artifacts with distinct, evidence-grounded judge roles.

Judges must inspect the profile interpretation, triggers, candidate evidence, status and deadline records, eligibility, effort, bridge, actionability analysis, ranking comparisons, and final report—not only polished prose. They may not browse or reward unsupported plausibility. A missing or unsupported status, deadline, or eligibility claim cannot be compensated by writing quality.

Run the following logically separate roles and return `evals/schemas/judge_result.schema.json` (and `evaluation.schema.json` where appropriate):

- **Evidence judge:** provenance, official-source support, liveness, eligibility discipline, and uncertainty hygiene.
- **Actionability judge:** causal bridge, seven-day first action, deliverable, effort, and practical strategic value.
- **Personalization/portfolio judge:** profile fit, context intersections, diversity, and ranking quality.
- **Pairwise judge:** only blinded A/B ordering under the hard gates.

Keep role ownership explicit, preserve raw and parsed results, cite artifact IDs for failures, and let an evidence hard failure override other praise. Do not add a serendipity requirement; novelty remains optional until V7.
