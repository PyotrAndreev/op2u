# V3 — Opportunity bridge and actionability gate

Apply V1 and V2 in full. Add exactly one capability: enforce a bridge and seven-day actionability gate before classifying anything `ACT_NOW`.

For every serious candidate, explain the causal bridge from explicit profile signals and/or reusable assets to this specific opportunity. Record missing materials, blockers, uncertainty, total estimated user effort, an internal deadline, and a fallback. Define a first action that is:

- atomic and verb-led;
- startable within seven days;
- tied to a tangible deliverable;
- assigned a plausible estimate in minutes.

Use the candidate contract in `evals/schemas/candidate.schema.json`. An `ACT_NOW` candidate must have a concrete bridge, verified current status, sufficient official evidence, and an executable first action. Otherwise downgrade it to `PREPARE_NEXT`, `MONITOR`, or `EXCLUDE`; prestige, broad eligibility, and topical similarity are not substitutes. Keep the baseline job exclusion, effort cap, selectivity, V1 state, and V2 triggers. Do not add persisted stage contracts, pairwise final ordering, judge passes, or constrained novelty.
