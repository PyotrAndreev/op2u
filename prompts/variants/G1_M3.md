# G1_M3 — Strict ACT_NOW liveness and low-friction gate

Parent: `V4`
Affected stage: `ranking` only

Apply V1–V4 in full. The behavior below is unchanged:

- **V1:** Make the user's dynamic state explicit before search. Separate durable facts, active trajectories, context, commitments, events, geography certainty, reusable assets, readiness, constraints, exclusions, open decisions, missing information, and assumptions. Never infer citizenship, work authorization, visa eligibility, legal status, compensation needs, or an unstated employment request; preserve uncertainty.
- **V2:** Produce non-empty context-trigger hypotheses connecting at least two independent profile signals. Use them as search directions alongside direct categories, while keeping triggers distinct from evidence and preserving all V1 exclusions.
- **V3:** For every serious candidate, preserve the causal bridge, assets, blockers, uncertainty, effort, internal deadline, fallback, and an atomic verb-led seven-day deliverable with a plausible minute estimate. `ACT_NOW` still requires a concrete bridge, verified current status, sufficient official evidence, and an executable first action; otherwise use `PREPARE_NEXT`, `MONITOR`, or `EXCLUDE`. Keep baseline job exclusion, selectivity, and the six-hour weekly effort gate.
- **V4:** Run the named persisted stages in order: `profile_interpretation`, `trigger_synthesis`, `search_plan`, `discovery`, `verification`, `actionability`, `ranking`, and `report`. Save each machine-readable artifact before using it, pass later stages only declared artifact references and content hashes, use only persisted facts in the final report, label inference/assumptions/unknowns, and preserve failed or partial stages. Keep all V1–V3 gates. Do not add pairwise ranking, judge-specific evaluation, or a serendipity reservation.

## Exactly one mutation: make `ACT_NOW` a strict ranking classification

Change only the `ranking` stage. Do not change discovery or verification collection, the actionability contract, the report contract, the baseline rubric, or any other V1–V4 behavior.

A candidate may be classified `ACT_NOW` only when all three added conditions hold:

1. **Verified liveness:** the saved verification artifact contains an official-source claim, with its exact quote, URL, and retrieval time, that explicitly supports a currently live actionable state at the snapshot (`open`, `accepting`, or an explicitly active submission window). A deadline alone, an unverified assertion of openness, a stale page, or an uncertain status does not satisfy this condition. Preserve an officially announced future opening as `PREPARE_NEXT` until the window is live.
2. **Specific why-now reason:** the ranking artifact contains a `why_now` sentence that names the verified current trigger—such as a live window, a verified closing date, or a time-bounded current eligibility/action window—and explains why delaying the first action loses a concrete present advantage. It must cite the verification claim reference; generic reasons such as “good fit,” “high value,” or “open source” do not qualify.
3. **Low-friction first action:** the saved actionability artifact contains one atomic, verb-led first action that produces a tangible draft, question, submission component, or other intermediate deliverable within seven days, is startable without resolving an unknown eligibility/legal/visa assumption, and is estimated at no more than 60 minutes. A multi-hour application, vague research task, or action blocked on an unverified prerequisite does not qualify as low friction.

If any condition fails, do not use `ACT_NOW`: downgrade to `PREPARE_NEXT` when a credible preparation path exists, otherwise use `MONITOR` or `EXCLUDE` under the unchanged V3/V4 rules. Save the condition results, `why_now` reason, verification claim reference, and first-action reference in the `ranking` artifact. The final report must copy the persisted classification and reason rather than upgrading a candidate in prose.
