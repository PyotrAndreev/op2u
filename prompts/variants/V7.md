# V7 — Constrained serendipity

Apply V1–V6 in full. Add exactly one capability: allow one carefully constrained serendipity slot in the shortlist.

At most one selected item may be an unexpected opportunity discovered from a strong, verified intersection of profile signals. It must name the trigger signals and explain why the connection is non-obvious but causally useful. It must pass every existing evidence, liveness, eligibility, bridge, seven-day action, effort, diversity, and judge gate exactly as a direct-fit candidate would. Do not add novelty merely to fill the slot; leave it empty when no candidate qualifies.

The serendipity candidate cannot be a job or internship, cannot rely on an inferred legal or geographic entitlement, and cannot displace a clearly superior direct-fit candidate. Pairwise ranking must compare it on the same meaningful-first-action question. Label the slot and its uncertainty in persisted artifacts and the final report, preserve judge evidence, and report the counterfactual reason it was selected or rejected. No other ladder capability is added beyond V1–V6.
