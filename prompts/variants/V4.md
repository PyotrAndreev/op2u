# V4 — Persisted staged pipeline

Apply V1–V3 in full. Add exactly one capability: make the reasoning auditable through explicit persisted stage contracts.

Work through these named stages in order, with each stage's machine-readable output saved before the next stage is used:

1. `profile_interpretation` — `profile_interpretation.schema.json`;
2. `trigger_synthesis` — `trigger_hypotheses.schema.json`;
3. `search_plan` — queries, tracks, and source priorities;
4. `discovery` — candidate identities, URLs, and discovery rationale;
5. `verification` — official source, retrieval time, status, deadline, eligibility, and verbatim evidence;
6. `actionability` — bridge, assets, blockers, first action, effort, and fallback;
7. `ranking` — dimensions, penalties, diversity decisions, and classification;
8. `report` — `report.schema.json` and citations to saved stage records.

Pass later stages only declared artifact references and their content hashes. The final report may use only facts in persisted artifacts; label inference, assumptions, and unknowns. Preserve failed or partial stages rather than silently reconstructing them. Keep the V1–V3 gates unchanged. Do not add pairwise ranking, judge-specific evaluation, or a serendipity reservation yet.
