# Target Audience

## Primary Audience
* international talents
* early/career professionals
* students / researchers
* creators / artists
* founders / makers
* NGO / policy / impact-oriented people
* people who want growth through mobility, funding, recognition, education, and community

---

## They Search
* jobs
* scholarships
* summer / tech schools
* grants
* travel opportunities
* conferences
* fellowships
* residencies
* accelerators
* hackathons
* research visits
* NGO / UN / policy programs
* open calls with travel or accommodation support

---

## Core User Need
* relevant opportunities
* deadline control
* auto application help
* status tracking
* profile improvement
* strategic choice between many possible paths

---

## Best Initial Audience
**International ambitious talent seeking funded mobility opportunities.**

Why this wedge is strong:
* high motivation
* fragmented supply
* real deadlines
* applications are repetitive
* discovery is hard
* users can clearly measure success

## Not First Priority

Avoid starting too broad.

Do **not** begin with:

* all jobs
* all education
* all grants
* all hobbies
* all countries
* all users

Start with:

> **funded international mobility for ambitious people**

Then expand into:

> **personal opportunity operating system**
> for career, education, funding, recognition, travel, and life growth.
