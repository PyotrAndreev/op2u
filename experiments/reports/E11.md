# E11 — Synthetic perturbations

**Master definition:** Synthetic isolated temporal/causal perturbations.

**Hypothesis:** Rankings respond monotonically to relevant changes and remain invariant to irrelevant changes.

**Primary readout:** Causal sensitivity, monotonicity, and irrelevant-field invariance.

**Status:** **not_run**. No persisted synthetic perturbation suite, paired inputs, or monotonicity/invariance results is present in the experiment inventory. Live-run variation cannot substitute for controlled perturbation testing, and no behavioral or holdout result is available.

A future E11 must alter one declared temporal or causal field at a time, preserve all other inputs and source records, and record the expected direction before judging.
