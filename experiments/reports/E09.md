# E09 — Executable action packet

**Master definition:** Action label only vs executable action packet.

**Hypothesis:** Action packets improve first-step executability.

**Primary readout:** Atomic action, deliverable, effort/blocker/internal-deadline completeness.

**Status:** **partial**. V4 and later reports contain concrete deliverables, minute estimates, and uncertainty notes, and the G2 judges accepted the actionability of the controlled replays. But the inventory has no matched label-only control, no isolated E09 assignment, and no complete packet-completeness comparison.

The frontier supports requiring atomic deliverables and effort fields. It does not support claiming a measured E09 improvement, general first-action behavior, or a significance result.
