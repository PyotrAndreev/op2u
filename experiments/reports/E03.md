# E03 — Event/change-driven search

**Master definition:** Category-first vs event/change-driven search.

**Hypothesis:** Event-driven search finds more temporally privileged opportunities.

**Primary readout:** Timing advantage, context intersections, liveness.

**Status:** **partial**. The staged ladder contains trigger and search-plan artifacts, and the reports include live/upcoming windows, but no matched category-first control or isolated repeated E03 cell is persisted. The observed live CFPs show that temporal verification matters; they do not measure a timing advantage or candidate recall.

Do not infer that event-driven search caused the observed opportunities: live pages, search variance, and cumulative prompt changes are confounded. A controlled E03 should hold profile, source access, and budget fixed while changing only search strategy and should score liveness and context intersections separately.
