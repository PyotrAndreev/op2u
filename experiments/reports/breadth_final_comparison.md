## Why BH7 won

BH7 did not maximize raw horizon length. It won by preserving only exact, primary-source-supported opportunity rows and moving attractive but unsupported paths to `exploration_leads`. The decisive mutation was implemented in the runner, not prose: raw model output is preserved, exact quote/URL/time rows are matched against verification, mismatches are removed, hashes are injected deterministically, unsupported ACT_NOW items are downgraded, effort is recomputed, and verified-family counts are recalculated.

## Remaining weaknesses

- No verified recurring contribution-capable South Africa adaptation route was found; adaptation score remained zero.
- The second Shanghai window is uncovered.
- The verified horizon has two families, below the target of four.
- Africa Tech Festival is a useful dated lead, but the retained exact evidence did not prove both current participation status and temporal trigger strongly enough for verified-horizon credit.
- Cost for compact text-mode calls is not provider-reported.
- No genuine holdout or behavioral outcome labels exist.

The result is the best observed breadth pipeline for this profile snapshot, not evidence of generalization or real-world conversion.
