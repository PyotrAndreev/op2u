# E08 — Grounded judging

**Master definition:** Report-only judge vs judge receiving profile, evidence, eligibility, effort, bridge, and artifacts.

**Hypothesis:** Grounded judging reduces prose/style bias and unsupported high scores.

**Primary readout:** Evidence hard failures, score shifts, and judge disagreement.

**Status:** **partial**. Persisted judges evaluated saved artifacts and identified concrete failures: V0's closed NLnet status/missing quotes, V4/G1_M3 effort failures, and later eligibility/format gaps. However, there is no matched report-only judge arm with a predeclared score-shift comparison. Role outputs also use inconsistent coverage, so their numeric scores cannot be naively pooled.

Grounded judging is supported as a process requirement; E08's effect size and causal claim are not established.
