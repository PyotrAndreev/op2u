# Generation 2 results

Initial full reruns showed substantial discovery variance, so they were not credited to the ranking mutation. Three controlled replays reused G1_M3 artifacts through actionability and executed only ranking/report.

All three controlled G2_M1 repeats had no hard failures. Conservative scheduled upper bounds were 150, 150, and 165 minutes. In blinded parent/child comparison the child received four votes and two ties with no losses; personalization was an order-stable child win while actionability/evidence changed from tie to child under reversal.

Decision: G2_M1 becomes provisional frontier; confidence is moderate because evidence detail remains variable and not every role comparison was order-stable.
