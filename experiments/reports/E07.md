# E07 — Pairwise shortlist tournament

**Master definition:** Independent 0–5 scoring vs pairwise shortlist tournament.

**Hypothesis:** Pairwise ranking improves top-3 stability.

**Primary readout:** Order stability, dominated selections, and A/B agreement.

**Status:** **partial**. Four blinded comparisons were persisted, including reversed presentation orders and role-specific results. This directly exercises pairwise evidence, but it does not compare an independent 0–5 tournament against a pairwise tournament on the same candidate pool with repeated top-3 stability as the primary endpoint.

The evidence is still operationally useful: `ae274aef40` was order-sensitive for two roles, while `0535929b15` was order-stable and unanimous 6/6. Therefore pairwise results should be reported with order stability, not treated as an unconditional ranking effect.
