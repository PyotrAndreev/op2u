# User-input gaps

The runs show that better ranking is often blocked by information that must remain unknown rather than inferred. The production intake should request, when the user can provide it:

- **Snapshot controls:** current date, timezone, and the date by which an opportunity must still be actionable.
- **Goal and route preference:** funding, visibility, collaborators, users, validation, or another explicitly prioritized outcome; tolerance for public speaking, applications, travel, and outreach.
- **Project state:** current prototype/repository status, public/private status, traction, named collaborators, and what can safely be reused as an asset.
- **Eligibility facts:** only facts the user chooses to disclose that affect a program's stated requirements. Do not fill gaps with assumptions about citizenship, residence, visa/work authorization, legal status, health, or compensation.
- **Geography and logistics:** preferred or feasible locations, remote constraints, travel ability, and relevant time windows.
- **Effort and timing:** the real weekly minute budget, fixed commitments, internal deadlines, and whether a first action can be scheduled now.
- **Deliverable preferences:** acceptable artifact type, desired level of polish, and whether an inquiry is preferable to a speculative submission.
- **Evidence permissions:** official pages or user-provided documents that may be checked, and any source-access restrictions.

Observed evidence gaps were concrete: official status/deadline quotes were sometimes present while eligibility or submission-format support was not. A claim such as “lightning talk” must therefore remain an uncertainty or be verified directly. The ledger must not manufacture missing support.

There is also a measurement gap: this evaluation has no genuine holdout and no behavioral outcomes such as opened, first-action, submitted, or successful. Those are not user-input facts that can be reconstructed from the reports. Treat the current result as proxy quality for this profile, not evidence of user conversion or generalization.
