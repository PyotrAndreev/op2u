# E02 — Trigger synthesis

**Master definition:** Direct profile search vs trigger synthesis before search.

**Hypothesis:** Trigger synthesis improves bridge recall and non-obvious discovery.

**Primary readout:** Valid triggers, bridge recall, candidate recall.

**Status:** **partial**, not a controlled isolated comparison. V1/V2 are cumulative single screens, and V2 adds trigger/context hypotheses while changing the overall prompt. Persisted trigger artifacts demonstrate that the stage ran, but they do not establish recall against a direct-search control.

The frontier evidence supports using trigger synthesis operationally, especially for dated windows and changes, but does not quantify E02. A valid test needs the same profile and research budget, direct versus synthesized trigger plans, candidate-pool preservation, and a predeclared recall criterion.
