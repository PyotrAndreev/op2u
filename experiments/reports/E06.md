# E06 — Budgeted discovery funnel

**Master definition:** Exhaustive research vs broad low-cost discovery followed by selective verification.

**Hypothesis:** A budgeted funnel improves actionable discoveries per cost without reducing top-3 quality.

**Primary readout:** Actionable candidates per token/cost and top-3 score.

**Status:** **partial**. Staged runs use discovery followed by verification and expose elapsed times, but no exhaustive-research control, token-normalized comparison, or isolated cost/readout cell is persisted. Provider usage is incomplete in the inventory; the final accounting reports provider cost for the suite but not a valid E06 efficiency estimate.

The funnel is recommended as an auditable design choice, not as a demonstrated cost improvement. Any future E06 must record comparable source counts, usage, cost, and top-3 quality under matched live conditions.
